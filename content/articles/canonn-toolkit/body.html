The Canonn Toolkit has been developed to provide useful tools while you are in-game, and is currently available for Windows users.

Current Version 1.02 provides:

  • Bearing calculator
  • Numbers to ASCII converter
  • Morse to ASCII converter
  • Keyword Cipher
  • Notebook

Further instructions and download links can be found on Github
