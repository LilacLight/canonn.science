An Unregistered Comms Beacon can be found in the Pru Aescs TY-J a64-1 system, orbiting body 1.

The transmission times of this beacon are on the hour, every hour.

Note: There are two separate UCB's approximately 20km's apart, however, both UCB's transmit the same message at the same transmission times.

The transmitted message is the following:

Unregistered Comms Beacon - Pru Aescs TY-J a64-1

DYJEPQY UWLUDCQCKJ XUEOKJ
DJPQY P6 UNL002
NEHHY LKCJQ 3
OKJSHRW
LNR EUPOP QYF E64 1
IKDUPQEJDXY

The UCB message is repeated two times before ending

Applying a Keyed Caesar cipher with the key of EXODUS yields the message:

Decoded UCB Message

DYNASTY EXPEDITION BEACON
DNSTY S6 ERP002
RALLY POINT 3
CONFLUX
PRU AESCS TYJ A64 1
MODESTANDBY

An abandoned settlement can be found within the same system on body 3.

The coordinates for the site are at 57.0706, 94.2092 (in Live/4.0) or at -66.4698, -56.1327 (in Legacy/3.8).

Around the settlement there are four Settlement Comms Log Uplink points, scanning them gives background to the reason for its abandonment:
