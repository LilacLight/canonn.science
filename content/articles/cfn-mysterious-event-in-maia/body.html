Canonn Faction News brings you footage taken shortly after Commanders Delmonte, Starbeaver and Hugo Ross from the Canonn Council discovered a distress beacon in Maia containing an assortment of ships damaged by as yet unknown assailants.  Cmdr Delmonte, first on the scene, reported sighting of an Unknown Ship leaving the area soon after dropping in on the beacon. CFN reporter Artimus Tau provides commentary on the damage observed on the ships. Note that due to the exceptional circumstances the video was captured under, audio is muted until 01:50
