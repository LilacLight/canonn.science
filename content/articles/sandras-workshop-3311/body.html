On the 15th December 3311, a Galnet was released from Sandra Corrs, CEO of Sandra’s Workshop, requesting help delivering gifts for the festive season.

Note: You will need either a Small or Medium sized ship, ideally with at least 8 or more cargo space.

Galnet: SANDRA’S WORKSHOP ASKS FOR FESTIVE GIFT COURIERS

15 DEC 3311

The CEO of leading children’s toy manufacturer Sandra’s Workshop has once again requested help with parcel deliveries.

Sandra Corrs, the much-loved philanthropist who provides gifts to children across the core systems each year, has made similar requests in previous years. Last year, with the Thargoid invasion just beginning, her courier fleet was loaned out to help refugee evacuation efforts.

This year a different problem has emerged, as Sandra explained to Vox Galactica:

“We had Our Dolph’s Routes transport fleet all prepped in advance this year, and with no Thargoids in sight, we thought we would finally have no more hiccups. Oh, how wrong we were!

This year, we’ve been hacked, and most of our location data for each present was wiped clean. We managed to restore the majority from a backup, but a few presents are going out that we still need help with figuring out where to deliver them.”

“Even our fleet won’t be able to deliver all our presents on time, given the extra work needed to track down their proper delivery points. We’ll need to rely once again on kind-hearted pilots to volunteer their time and ensure we can brighten the smiles on every child on our list.”

Pilots willing to help Sandra’s Workshop can collect festive gifts from the Frost Dock outpost in the Njambalba system. The outpost can only provide landing pads for small- and medium-sized vessels.

A beacon outside Frost Dock holds delivery information, which can be acquired via scan.

Galnet: Sandra’s Workshop Asks for Festive Gift Couriers

Upon visiting the Njambalba system and then docking at the Frost Dock outpost, you will be greeted with an Inbox message from Sandra Corrs, and just under 9.5 km away there is a Listening Post named “Sandra’s Postbox” which contains letters from the children requesting gifts.

While you are docked, make sure to purchase some “Festive Gifts” which is a rare commodity available at the outpost.

You will need to purchase at least 8 Festive Gifts, as you will need to sell 1 Festive Gift to each station (listed below) to be able to complete the event.

MESSAGE FROM SANDRA CORRS

Season’s greetings!

Thank you for visiting Frost Dock, Commander. It’s such a busy time of year here, and we’re in need of your help, again.

Outside this station is a beacon containing letters from children all across the bubble. Someone who is now on my naughty list has removed the delivery instructions from some of the kids’ messages!

If you haven’t already, scan the beacon to obtain a selection of letters. You’ll have to figure out where the sender lives from the message text – it can sometimes be confusing.

Don’t forget to collect Festive Gifts from the commodity market at Frost Dock first – I’ll make sure the appropriate gifts are included. For your help, I’ve arranged for a selection of fine festive rewards with each confirmed delivery.

Have a wonderful holiday season!
Sandra

Gunther’s letter gives us a couple of clues to find his location, “big monkey” and his brothers name, “Harry“.

While searching the Galaxy Map for “Monkey” will not get any results for stations, and searching for “Harry” will only return a single result, if we instead search for “Big” we get many more results, including one named “Big Harry’s Monkey Hangout” in the Jotunheim system.

Upon travelling to Big Harry’s Monkey Hangout in the Jotunheim system and delivering at least one Festive Gift, you will receive an inbox message with the following:

Ari’s letter mentions “three wise men who followed a star” and “Our dock name is like one of the presents carried by the three wise men.“.

On ancient Earth, there was a tale about three wise men who followed a star and then gave gifts to a young child – the gifts being Gold, Frankincense, and Myrrh. Searching the Galaxy Map for Frankincense, and Myrrh will not return any results, however searching for Gold will quite a few.

To help narrow down our destination, Ari also mentioned “Dock“, his Dad’s name “Tom“, along with the families surname “Ani” at the start of the letter.

Looking through the list of results, there is a Gold Dock in the Tomani system, which seems to match up with the clues.

Upon travelling to Gold Dock in the Tomani system and delivering at least one Festive Gift, you will receive an inbox message with the following:

Sophie letter mentions “Aunty Carol read me a Christmas story last week about a mean old man. But he learned about being kind and generous at Christmas time. The mean old man was visited by a spirit friend in chains who said three more ghosts would visit him.“.

A number of clues here point towards another ancient Earth tale, “A Christmas Carol“. In the story, an elderly man named Ebenezer Scrooge, is visited by the ghost of his former business partner Jacob Marley. If we search the Galaxy Map with the surname of the former business partner, Marley, then we find that there are several possible results.

To help narrow down our destination, Sophie also mentioned stated that “The spirit friend is named after the base where I live!“. If we combine Marley and Base in our search, we find that there is a single result for a “Marley Base” in the Guan Gong system.
