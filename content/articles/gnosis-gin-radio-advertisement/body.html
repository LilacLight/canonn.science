Long ago, deep inside a condemned wing of Thompson Dock, in a forgotten laboratory, a lonely scientist accidentally distilled his beloved pet bog spaniel. In his grief he jumped from the nearest airlock, never to be seen again. Little did he know that he had brought the galaxy a delicious new beverage: Gnosis Gin.

Canonn Interstellar have refined the recipe and added extra bog spaniel for that rich, oily taste in every mouthful.
