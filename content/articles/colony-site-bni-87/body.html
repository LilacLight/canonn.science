Colony Site BNI-87 is an abandoned settlement that can be found in the Wregoe TJ-Z d6 system, on body B 1.

The coordinates for the site are at -35.6729, -53.7410 (in Live/4.0) or at -65.2125, -78.2328 (in Legacy/3.8).

This site was first reported by Commander Viaharo.

The location of this site can be found by following the three nearby listening posts listed below.
