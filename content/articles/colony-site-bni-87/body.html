Colony Site BNI-87 is an abandoned settlement in the Wregoe TJ-Z d6 system on body B 1, at -35.6729, -53.7410 (in Live/4.0) or at -65.2125, -78.2328 (in Legacy/3.8).

This site was first reported by Commander Viaharo.
