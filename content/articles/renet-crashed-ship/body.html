A crashed Anaconda can be found in Renet system, on body B 1, at -34.7043 / 82.6290 (in Live/4.0) or at 13.9374 / 135.0855 (in Legacy/3.8).

Claimed to have been found by CMDR NME Darkrevenge.

A log can be found at the site by scanning the ship data core.

Scanned Message

Comms Record:

No one is going to look for this stuff here. We’ll come back for it in a couple of weeks once the dust settles, and then it’s the good life for us.

Note: This site one of many sites that are known as “Tip Off” sites, meaning that they are usually discovered by following a message from the “Mysterious Stranger” and this type of site will only appear for a short duration (active / visible for 2 weeks, before going dormant / non-visible for 10 weeks).
