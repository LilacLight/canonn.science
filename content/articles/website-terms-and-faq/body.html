This group, and the website

  • The Canonn is an established player faction in the Elite Dangerous universe comprising many players from across the world.
  • We’re focused on enjoyment and cooperation in-game, and/or on this site, on Discord, and/or on the Frontier Forums.
  • There are people here that only ever play Solo in the Canonn. There are people that only ever play in Open. There are PvP players and PvE players – all are welcome.
  • And of course: gender, race, religion, sexuality or nationality are not important to us either 🙂
  • We’re not concerned with the other factions or powers to whom you might belong, although we do not welcome ‘griefers‘ at all.

‘Griefing’ – What is it?

  • Griefers are not pirates who kill targets when their demands for cargo fail to be met by the victim.
  • Griefers are not PvPers who attack other combat fit CMDRs either for bounty, or in a Conflict Zone.
  • Griefers are explorer-killers, or noob-killers, so long as the noob did not instigate the fight or matches any of the above criteria.

The Council/Moderation

  • The Canonn has a representative group of individual commanders called the Council that is appointed entirely by self-nomination. We make no secret of that.
  • The initial core council has grown by selecting members of the community whom they feel best represent the interests of the group and the players within it. The Council are not power-crazy – we see our role as offering direction to the group but also looking after its interests.
  • The ‘Council’ members not only perform decision-making roles and (a form of) leadership for the group, but are also de-facto moderators of this site.
  • Site moderators can delete and edit any content and can approve and block users.
  • We also have forum moderators who are ‘normal’ members.

Registering

  • By registering on this site, you accept to remain respectful at all times to your fellow group members, both here, on the forums, in the game, and on Discord. That doesn’t mean you can’t disagree with people – it’s just about respect.
  • We reserve the right to refuse or revoke access to this site and the group, without needing to justify ourselves.
  • In-game activities contrary to our griefing policy, however, will – if reported and verified – almost certainly see your account deleted and access to Discord and the Private Group rescinded.
  • Also, misrepresentation of our group on social media, on the frontier forums, reddit, other faction group sites can also lead to your account being rescinded.
  • We have both a public research policy, and a beta research policy which govern how we expect our ‘research’ of in-game mysteries to be shared by our members and associates (other groups or non-Canonn commanders).  You must abide by these at all times in any of our communications channels.
  • When you say you represent the Canonn, you’re also representing the many hundreds of other members 😉 Our philosophy is fun and cooperation, not bullying or griefing.

Financial

  • Both the group and this website are not-for-profit, and access/participation will always be free-of-charge. The website is maintained and developed specifically for the benefit of members of the group and the wider Elite Dangerous community.
  • While we are not-for-profit, it does cost money to run – so please consider donating:
  • The money goes straight to the site’s principal developer into a fund that is used to pay website costs, and for things like prizes, etc. Please don’t set up recurring payments (as much as that kind of support is very welcome!) as they don’t work on our PayPal account.

Discord and Private Group

  • Access to our Private Group requires access to our Discord channel.
  • You don’t need to be a member of these, though, to register on the site or to be given member status.
  • While it would be great to have the group completely open to all, the reality is we just can’t do this.  The Private Group is intended as a walled garden for safe exploration/experimentation and only used for elective PvP – Mobius is a similar (and wonderful) place, but with so many members we can’t guarantee players’ safety in there. Indeed, during the Barnacle explosion, there some instances of griefing within Mobius.  We understand those players have since been removed from Mobius.
  • Many Canonn CMDRs continue to play in open, however.

Security/Your data

  • Your data on this site will never be sold or shared with anyone. Ever.
  • The Moderators/Council are able to view your email address, but it will never be published anywhere.
  • Any Councillor or Moderator found to be misusing this information will be blocked and ejected from the group, and will be named and shamed.
  • Your user name, Frontier forum name and CMDR name will be displayed in our directory, accessible only to other registered and approved users.
  • Your password is stored in our database in a way that ensures it can’t be reverse-engineered.
  • The site does not use SSL – it might in the future – but this means that any untrustworthy internet intermediaries (proxies etc) you use to access the site could capture your password. We consider the risk of this to be very small – but will continue to review this as the site grows.
