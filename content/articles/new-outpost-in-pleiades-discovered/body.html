Cmdr Panpiper reports in the Canonn Discord and in the Threadnought of finding a new Merope Expeditionary Fleet planetary outpost named Orcus Crag on Planet A3 of system Pleiades Sector OI-T C3-7, nearly 60 light years from Merope. While taking note of Imperial interest in the area, Cmdr Panpiper located 3 Barnacles in the system, which so far appear to be the closest found to the Bubble and the furthest found from the nebula.
