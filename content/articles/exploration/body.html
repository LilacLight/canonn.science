The Search for Jaques Station

Around 26 May 3302 Jaques Station disappears after an unsuccessful jump to from Gliese 1269 to Beagle Point. However, it was reported that outposts received incomplete messages from the station indicating that Jaques is stranded somewhere in the Galaxy. Jaques has been found, but is there something special about his new home Eol Prou RS-T d3-94, 12000 ly away from Sol?
