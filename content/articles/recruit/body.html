Originally drawn together from the four corners of inhabited space to investigate the Unknown Artefacts, The Canonn consists of Commanders from all professions, all allegiances, and all walks of life. One common goal unites them: to seek the truth behind the galaxy’s mysteries at any cost.

Do you have what it takes to join the Tin Foil Berets?

  1. Register an account to this site – Be sure to include your in-game CMDR name in your application.
  2. Introduce yourself – Create a post introducing yourself in our ‘New Users’ forum, saying who you are (RL or RP, it’s up to you!) and what your favourite biscuit is.
  3. SCIENCE!! – Once you’ve completed the first two steps, someone will do some behind-the-scenes magic that will give you access to this page and the rest of the site. This page contains our Discord instructions & Membership directory as well as other information.
