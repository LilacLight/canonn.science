After continuing detailed study of the site at HIP 17044 Canonn Research is confident in revealing further information about the incident. Cmdr CrescentMage and Cmdr Tanall noticed that from one of the Farraguts there seemed to be some sort of EM discharge that was being picked up in the audio band. This turned out to be a time delayed radio transmission, an encrypted modulated sound which Cmdr AdmlAdama was able to slow down, isolate and extract a hidden Morse Code message revealing the unfortunate ship’s final moments.
