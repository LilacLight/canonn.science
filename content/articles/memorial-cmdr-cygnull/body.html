A memorial dedicated to CMDR Cygnull can be found in the NLTT 52560 system, orbiting body A 4.

Upon scanning the Listening Post, the memorial reads:

COMMANDER CYGNULL

Play your cards,
Go against all odds,
Shoot for the moon; if you miss,
You're still among the stars
RIP Commander Cygnull 5/7/3304
