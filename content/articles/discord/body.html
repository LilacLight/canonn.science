Discord is the main way the members of Canonn talk with each other. It is where research is discussed, finds are shared, expeditions and events are organised and the latest news arrives first. If you want to see what Canonn is really like, it is the best place to start.

Join the Canonn Discord server at canonn.fyi/discord.

Visitors are welcome

You do not have to be a member of Canonn to visit the server. Visitors can read everything that goes on in the laboratories, so you can follow the discussions, see how the research is done and get a feel for the group before you decide whether to join. There are also a few public channels where non-members can talk.

Only full members can talk in the labs

To take part in the conversations in the laboratories you need to become a full member of Canonn. In the words of the #newcomers_welcome channel: you are more than welcome to listen in on our conversations, but if you want to participate you will need to become one of us.

You are free to be a member of other factions or powers, but we ask you not to bring external drama into Canonn.

Becoming a member

Membership is quick and free, and starts on this website. The steps are:

  1. Read the #server_rules channel and the Welcome pack.
  2. Post a message in the #newcomers_welcome channel with the colour you would like for your username. A Canonn moderator will then give you the right roles and you will have access to the full server.

Finding your way around

New arrivals will find the notices at the top of the server: the #server_rules, #community and #useful_links channels. Below them are the channels for galactic affairs such as #news and #galnet, and then the laboratories themselves.

Discord is great for live chat and keeping up with the latest news, but it is not a good place to store data. Canonn's lasting research is kept on this website. We would like to encourage you to write your own articles for publishing your results.
