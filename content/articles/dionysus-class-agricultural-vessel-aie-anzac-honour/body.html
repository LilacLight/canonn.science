Dionysus Class Agricultural Vessel AIE Anzac Honour is a megaship orbiting Pleiades Sector GW-W c1-15 11 which appears to have been added as a result of a Community Goal for Angeli Imperial Enterprises announced in galnet on 15 DEC 3303. There are materials floating around the area, and many security vessels which will scan you. There is a ship uplink which gives data.
