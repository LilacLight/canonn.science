This page tracks the currently expected locations of the following Megaships with known repeating Flight Operations Plans.

Dove Enigma - Dove Enigma is a dockable megaship in Colonia around 3 a, added to Colonia as part of the Enigma Expedition.
Fisher's Rest - The Flight Operations Megaship “Fisher's Rest” was last reported in the Aldebaran system, around the primary star.
Foster Terminal - The Flight Operations Megaship “Foster Terminal” was last reported in orbit of the planet Coeus A 2.
Oath of Tyndareus - The Oath of Tyndareus is a retrofitted Hogan-class Hauler.
The Alexandria - The Alexandria was a megaship owned by Aegis, the wrackage of which can be found in the Wregoe TC-X b29-0 system, in the planetary rings of body AB 2.
The Harmony - The Empyrean Foundation's Flight Operations Megaship “The Harmony” was last reported in orbit of the planet Yum Kamcabi A 11.
The Indra - The Flight Operations Megaship “The Indra” was last reported in Delphi orbiting body 5 a.
The Midas - The Flight Operations Megaship “The Midas” was last reported in orbit of the planet Zeus 1.
Tiliala's Lament - Tiliala's Lament is a Megaship introduced into the Akandinigua system around planet 5 when the Conservatives of Tiliala were displaced from their home system.
The Gnosis - The ship was constructed by an appeal to the wider community, and provides docking, repair, outfitting, and market services to all.
