A wrecked Anaconda can be found in the Leucos, on body A 5 a.

The coordinates for the site are at -36.1358, -45.2314

At the crash site, the ship's cargo has been scattered behind the wreckage, which includes Brewer Corp and Caine Massey crates as well as a number of Salvage cargo canisters from the following types:

  • Prototype Tech
  • Assault Plans
  • Tactical Data
  • Black Box
  • Escape Pods
  • Personal Effects
  • Military Intelligence
  • Technical Blueprints
  • Wreckage Components

The wreckage includes a Cargo Racks that can be scanned, but give no information or data, and shot which gives Raw Engineer Materials.

Within the crashed ship itself a Ship Data Core can be scanned for an Intel Package.
