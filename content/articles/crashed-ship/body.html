A wrecked Anaconda crashed nose-first into the moon Leucos A 5 a at -36.1280, -45.2436, scattering its cargo behind it. The wreckage, which includes Brewer Corp and Caine Massey crates as well as a number of Salvage cargo canisters from the following types:

  • Prototype Tech
  • Assault Plans
  • Tactical Data
  • Black Box
  • Escape Pods
  • Personal Effects
  • Military Intelligence
  • Technical Blueprints
  • Wreckage Components

The wreckage includes a Cargo Racks that can be scanned, but give no information or data, and shot which gives Raw Engineer Materials.

Within the crashed ship itself a Ship Data Core can be scanned for an Intel Package.

The location of this crash site is provided by a Galnet article in the local Herald:
