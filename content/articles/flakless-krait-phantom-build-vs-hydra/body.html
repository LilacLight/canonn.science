This video represents the first medium-ship Hydra kill with regular ammo.  This is also the first “Flakless” medium-ship Hydra kill.

The Krait Phantom (as well as the Mk2 and Python) has a special place among medium ships in that it can fire 3 medium Gauss Cannons and remain stealthed when running cold.  This is thanks to its large Class 7 power distributor.  When the PD is engineered to be Weapon Focused, the sheer size of the WEP capacitor allows for minimal heat generation.  Additionally, given the size of the PD, one can almost perma-boost in the Krait Phantom, even with a Weapon Focused PD.

Build Link: https://s.orbis.zone/4lpl

CMDR Maligno used these two amazing attributes to dispatch a Hydra Interceptor while using only basic ammo and no Flak Launcher. The large PD allowed him to stay stealthy while firing 3 Gauss Cannons and to boost away on demand in order to avoid the Swarm. Going Flakless may appear a bit of a punishing thing to do, but it can be quite liberating because the enrage timer is no longer an issue—the Interceptor can enrage all it wants.

It took a while to bring the Hydra down due to regular ammo and the limited DPS of 3 Gauss Cannons, but in the interest of transparency no parts were cut out of the fight. CMDR Maligno started with 69 reloads of basic ammo and finished with 45.  He was never really concerned he would run out, so that’s a major plus of fighting with basic ammo.

During the fight, the only time he encountered significant trouble was with the second heart. It took him over 30 minutes to finally catch a break, a mixture of nerves and silly flying prevented him from finishing it off quickly. The rest of the fight was much smoother.
